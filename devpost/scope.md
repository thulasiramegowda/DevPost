# Project Scope: ReviewLens

## What It Is
An AI-powered review trust checker for online shoppers. The shopper inputs a product URL from an e-commerce platform and receives an instant review trust report featuring an explainable 0–100 Trust Score, 6 deterministic risk signals, interactive charts, and an AI-generated synthesis of customer feedback.

## What It Is Not
- Not an e-commerce marketplace or storefront
- No user authentication or accounts
- No seller management or admin moderation panels
- No unauthorized live web scraping (uses realistic curated seed databases for demo reliability)

## Target Audience
Online shoppers (represented by persona Riya) who want quick, transparent insight into whether high star ratings are genuine or manipulated.
